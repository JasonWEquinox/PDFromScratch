## Niche shapes that work

1. **B2B add-on sold through a large platform's marketplace (Shopify, Zendesk, monday, Atlassian).** Strongest evidence in this run: Zigpoll (about $125K MRR, solo, 2026), Swifteq (about $51K/mo, solo, 2025), Pioneera ($30K MRR, vendor-reported), and Atlassian's 3-person and single-person vendors. The marketplace handles distribution and billing. Platform economics have improved (Shopify charges 0% on the first $1M lifetime, Atlassian takes 5% in year one). The risks are platform cannibalisation (Swifteq lost about $2K MRR to a native Zendesk feature) and crowding in the biggest stores (Shopify median price $9.99, 55% of apps with zero reviews). Read: this shape works best on **mid-size B2B platforms** (Zendesk, monday, Atlassian, likely HubSpot and Salesforce, the last unverified here) and is harder on Shopify.
2. **Back-office tool for agencies or operators who manage many units, priced per unit.** WP Umbrella reports $110K MRR at €1.99 per site; per-unit pricing multiplies revenue per customer. Evidence: 2 founder-controlled sources. The team is 7, so a 1–3 person version would sit lower.
3. **Vertical SaaS for one trade or regulated service provider** (home improvement, cleaning, Australian NDIS disability-service providers). Indie Hackers listings show $60K–$250K MRR, but most of these stories are probably older than 18 months, so this shape is plausible but **not freshly evidenced** in this run.
4. **Products priced above about $250/month or sold on annual plans.** ChartMogul's data shows retention improves sharply with price and annual billing, which supports the "meaningful revenue per customer" filter.

## Shapes that look saturated

1. **Thin AI wrappers / low-price consumer AI tools.** ChartMogul puts AI-native median GRR at 40% and NRR at 48%, with about 23% GRR under $50/month (secondary). TechCrunch reported Google VP's warning. Note that the "90% fail" statistics are unsourced.
2. **Generic Shopify merchant apps (AOV, upsell, bundles).** About 22.5K apps, about 85 new a day, median price $9.99, more than half with zero reviews. A Shopify community post reports that half the top AOV apps cost $10/month or less.
3. **No-code / drag-and-drop builders.** Predicted to see valuations compress from oversupply (one DEV opinion piece, low confidence).
4. **Generic micro-SaaS overall.** One valuation article reports a median of only about $145 MRR, which shows the survivorship bias in the success stories (low confidence on the exact number).

## Candidate gaps

| # | Opening suggested by evidence | Not small/boring (rev/customer) | Not crowded | Buildable by 1–3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Add-on apps for mid-size B2B platform marketplaces that are less crowded than Shopify** (Zendesk, monday, Atlassian Cloud, possibly HubSpot, Pipedrive, ServiceNow): workflow, reporting and QA gaps the platform has not filled | Medium–high (B2B seats; Swifteq at tens of thousands per month across about 300+ customers implies roughly $70–150 per customer per month, an inference) | Medium; per-app competition is unmeasured, and the core vendor may build the feature natively | Yes, proven solo (Swifteq, Pioneera) |
| 2 | **Per-unit back-office tools for agencies or MSPs** (sites, client accounts, locations, devices) outside WordPress, e.g. Webflow, Shopify-agency, or local-SEO multi-location ops | High via per-unit pricing | WordPress is taken (WP Umbrella, ManageWP); other ecosystems are unverified | Yes |
| 3 | **Vertical workflow plus compliance tool for one regulated service trade** (NDIS-style disability funding, trade certification renewal tracking, clinic equipment tracking) | High ($99–$299/month is typical; low-confidence source) | Likely, in sub-verticals too small for VC-backed players | Yes, but needs domain access; sales are slower |
| 4 | **Advanced Shopify B2B or Plus-tier apps** priced well above the $9.99 median, as Zigpoll did by "doubling down on the right segment" | High if aimed at Plus merchants | Generic Shopify is crowded; the Plus/B2B segment is less evidenced | Yes (Zigpoll is solo) |
| 5 | **AI features embedded in a vertical or marketplace workflow at more than $250/month** rather than a standalone wrapper | High | Thin wrappers are crowded; embedded vertical AI is less so (opinion-level evidence) | Yes, but inference cost and model risk apply |
